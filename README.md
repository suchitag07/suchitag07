## Hi there!

My name is Suchita Ganesan, and I work as a Project Assistant at the Braskie Lab at USC's Stevens Neuroimaging and Informatics Institute. My work involves processing multi-modal neuroimaging data for a range of research projects related to understanding brain aging and neurodegeneration. Most of my work and projects here focus on testing and building imaging pipelines for PET and fMRI data. I am always eager to go under the hood of various tools/software to understand how choices in image processing influence biological interpretation and statistical inference!
