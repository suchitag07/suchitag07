## Hi there!

My name is Suchita, and I currently work as a Project Assistant at USC's Stevens Neuroimaging and Informatics Institute with Dr. Meredith Braskie. My role involves processing multimodal neuroimaging data for a range of research projects focused on understanding brain health, aging, and neurodegeneration.

Most of my work here focuses on testing and building imaging pipelines for PET and fMRI data. I work primarily in Python, R, and MATLAB. I’m especially interested in going under the hood of different imaging software to understand how choices in image processing can influence biological interpretation and statistical inference.
