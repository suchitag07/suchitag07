## Hi there!

My name is Suchita Ganesan, and I work as a Project Assistant at the Braskie Lab at USC's Stevens Neuroimaging and Informatics Institute. My work involves processing multi-modal neuroimaging data for a range of research projects related to understanding brain health/aging and neurodegeneration. Most of my projects here focus on testing and building imaging pipelines for PET and fMRI data. I'm always eager to go under the hood of various software to understand how choices in image processing influence biological interpretation and statistical inference!
